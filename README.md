**Business Ledger** is an enterprise-grade, modern, and secure Android business management application built for retailers, wholesalers, merchants, and small-to-medium enterprises (SMEs). It streamlines daily bookkeeping, sales billing, customer & supplier due ledgers, stock inventory, and verified merchant networking in one unified, high-performance platform.

---

## 🌟 Key Application Features

### 1. 👥 Multi-User Management & Phone Authentication
- **Multi-Tenant Account Architecture:** Create, authenticate, and seamlessly switch between multiple business and staff accounts on a single device.
- **Merchant Profile Branding:** Customize business identity with store name, business category, trade license tags, bio, physical address, and communication channels.
- **Unique `@username` Merchant Identity:** Every merchant receives a global unique handle (`@username`) for direct merchant-to-merchant discovery and interaction.
- **Curated Business Avatars:** High-resolution industry-specific avatar illustrations for distinct shop branding.
- **Global Merchant Directory:** Search and discover registered businesses by username, shop name, or category with verified badges, store ratings, customer feedback, and lifetime sales metrics.
- **In-App Merchant Messaging:** Built-in live business communication with pre-formatted quick-action templates for payment dues, rate queries, and stock inquiries.

---

### 2. 📊 Executive Dashboard & Financial Analytics
- **At-a-Glance Financial KPI Cards:** Real-time summary of Total Sales, Total Receivables (Customer Due), Total Payables (Supplier Due), and Net Cash-in-Hand.
- **Today's Operational Breakdown:** Today's gross revenue, daily operational expenses, and net profit margins.
- **Interactive Sales Trend Charts:** Dynamic daily and weekly visual bar and line graphs showing revenue progression and sales velocity.
- **Quick Action Bar:** One-tap triggers for new invoices, expense entries, due collection records, and stock restocking.

---

### 3. 🛒 Sales POS, Digital Billing & Invoicing
- **Rapid Digital Invoicing:** Create itemized cash memos with customer details, multiple line items, unit prices, custom discounts, and regional tax/VAT rates.
- **Flexible Settlement Options:** Process instantaneous full cash settlements, partial down-payments, or 100% credit/due transactions.
- **Invoice Sharing & Printing:** Export and dispatch formatted digital receipts directly via WhatsApp, messaging apps, email, or wireless thermal Bluetooth receipt printers.

---

### 4. 📒 Customer & Supplier Due Ledger (Khata)
- **Customer Due Tracking (Receivables):** Granular customer-level ledgers recording historical transactions, outstanding dues, and due dates.
- **Supplier Payable Ledger:** Comprehensive procurement accounts tracking wholesale credits, scheduled payoffs, and delivery receipts.
- **Automated 1-Tap Reminders:** Generate personalized due collection notifications and dispatch them directly via SMS or WhatsApp.
- **Partial & Bulk Payments:** Record flexible installment repayments with automatic real-time balance recalculation.

---

### 5. 📦 Real-Time Stock & Inventory Tracking
- **Catalog Management:** Organize items with product SKU, purchase price, selling price, unit types (Pcs, Kg, Ltr, Box, etc.), and current shelf quantity.
- **Intelligent Low-Stock Alerts:** Automatic visual alerts and notifications when item quantities fall below configurable threshold safety levels.
- **Stock Audit Logs:** Complete chronological audit trail of stock-in receipts, sales deductions, and inventory adjustments.

---

### 6. 💸 Daily Expenses & Cash Book
- **Categorized Expense Records:** Record operational costs under customizable heads including Shop Rent, Utility Bills, Employee Payroll, Logistics, and Refreshments.
- **Cash Flow Reconciliation:** Daily ledger of cash-in versus cash-out with automatic closing balance calculations.

---

### 7. 📑 Financial Reports & Statement Export
- **Comprehensive Reporting:** Generate daily, weekly, monthly, and yearly Profit & Loss (P&L) statements, sales summaries, and due analysis.
- **Document Export:** Generate clean, print-ready PDF statements and spreadsheet-compatible data formats for tax and auditing purposes.

## 🛠️ Technical Stack & Architecture

- **Language:** 100% Kotlin
- **UI Framework:** Jetpack Compose (Material Design 3 - M3 Design System)
- **Design Architecture:** MVVM (Model-View-ViewModel) with Clean Architecture principles
- **Local Database:** Android Room ORM with SQLite & Kotlin Symbol Processing (KSP)
- **Reactive Concurrency:** Kotlin Coroutines, StateFlow, and SharedFlow
- **Navigation:** Jetpack Navigation Compose with type-safe routing contracts
- **Security & Persistence:** Encrypted Shared Preferences & local offline-first storage
- **Build System:** Gradle Kotlin DSL (`build.gradle.kts`) with Version Catalog (`libs.versions.toml`)

1. **Navigate to GitHub Repository:** Open the repository in your browser and click on the **Actions** tab.
2. **Trigger Build:** Select the **Android CI Build** workflow and click **"Run workflow"** (or **"Re-run all jobs"**).
3. **Download APK:** Once the workflow finishes successfully, scroll down to the **Artifacts** section and download the **`app-debug-apk`** ZIP bundle.
4. **Install on Device:** Extract the ZIP file to get `app-debug.apk` and install it on your Android smartphone.

> ℹ️ **Installation Tip:** If your phone displays *"App not installed as package conflicts with an existing package"*, simply uninstall any previously installed version of the app from your phone first, then proceed with installing the newly downloaded APK.
